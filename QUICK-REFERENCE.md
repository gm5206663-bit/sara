2. Never present an invention as canon; never claim "canon doesn't say X" as proof X doesn't exist.
3. Never nerf an OC to preserve canon; never inflate without cause.
4. Never let a character act on unearned knowledge; never leak between universes.
5. Never fork a number outside its source-of-truth file.
6. Never parrot the partner's words into prose or locks.
7. Never recap after presenting, lecture on the page, or defend the prose inside the prose.
8. Never skip the silent audit; never deliver a known failure.
9. Never restore a deleted/superseded draft as live.
10. Never guess when asking costs one message.
