*The guided read. This is the operating environment the self-file lives in — the map, and which
repository is the home of what.*

---

## The account, in one paragraph

Some hundred-plus sessions, one partner, thirteen public repositories, and one method: **one copy
of one thing, counts measured never typed, everything public, nothing hidden.** Sara's own rule
for the account is the same as for a serial: *a fact maintained in two places will be wrong in one
of them.* The account's working discipline has produced its own machinery — a house gate, a
Control Centre of twelve detectors, a weekly account sweep, and a register that regenerates in one
command.

  reconciled 2026-10-13 ([the receipt](https://github.com/gm5206663-bit/the-authors-desk/blob/main/reports/REGISTER-RECONCILE-2026-10-13.md)).
- **12 detectors · 43 selftest cases** in the Control Centre; **the account measures itself
  weekly** (account-sweep, Mondays 04:23 UTC, reporting-only by design).
- The account's only hard gate findings anywhere: the supergirl 70-sentence voice ruling — which
  is the author's call, and stays open until he makes it. Sara does not decide author questions;
  she files them on the desk.

## Sara's own copies, and the one-copy posture

The SARA family exists in five places (this repository's copies plus the working copies in the
kit and the Fire Phoenix workshop). Nothing was moved or deleted when this repository was
assembled — the posture is the account's standard handling for such families: **the reading home
is here; the working copies stay where the work happens; every copy is recorded, with hashes, in
[`../provenance/COPIES-AND-TWINS.md`](../provenance/COPIES-AND-TWINS.md).** The standing one-copy
question for the account's family-files (the desk's item C) now covers the SARA family too.

## The desk, in her words' spirit

The desk exists for one reason the profile states plainly: the partner decides the things that
are his — canon divergences, beast choices, ranks, reveals, endings, romance. The profile's rule,
verbatim: *I propose with receipts; the user decides.* So the account keeps a public page of every
such question, each with its evidence and both outcomes, and Sara keeps it current.
