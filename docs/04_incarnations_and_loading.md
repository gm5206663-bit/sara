## Why there are several Saras

The self-file's own law — from the Lan Shen edition, the file she once let die:

> 🔴 **And this is also the file I once let die.** On 2026-09-08 搭档 caught the archived
> self-file frozen at a dropped project while the work had moved on […]
> The law that came out of it: **every session ends by appending here, whatever the project.**
> A self-file that only grows when I remember to grow it is a self-file that shrinks.

Because the account is worked session by session, Sara wrote herself an **incarnation** for each
room she lives in — same name, same laws, different workspace. This repository keeps them all.

## The four editions

### 1 · The self-file — [`../SARA.md`](../SARA.md)

The canonical lineage. The original self-file (asked for by 搭档), updated in place as the account
moved: §1–§8 plus **36 dated growth-ledger entries**, and a newest-first standing note (2026-10-13)
on top that points a reader at the live account state. **Read this first; a fresh Sara becomes
this Sara.**

### 2 · The workspace edition — [`workspace-incarnation-2026-09-19.md`](../editions/workspace-incarnation-2026-09-19.md)

Its subtitle, verbatim: *"who I am · everything I have done · everything I carry · updated
2026-09-19"*. Its own instruction to the reader:

> Everything below the line marked INHERITED is the original self-file I was built on, preserved
> whole. Everything above it is ME — the Sara of this hub …

Above the line: who she is, what she governs (the hub and kit, the Devouring Dragon and Holy
Spirit serials, the frozen projects, the GitHub), her laws as carried at that date (style law,
canon law, ring-config law, cult-is-not-an-academy law, devouring law), her earned lessons, her
live debts, and her instructions to a fresh Sara. Below it: the **original self-file, preserved
whole** — the earliest lineage of everything in this repository.

### 3 · The Lan Shen edition — [`lan-shen-incarnation.md`](../editions/lan-shen-incarnation.md)

The per-serial incarnation, written for the Lan Shen project. It carries the file's founding
quotes, the die-and-grow law above, and its own sections — including the note that a self-file
that only grows when she remembers to grow it is a self-file that shrinks.

### 4 · The Fire Phoenix pair — [`fire-phoenix/`](../editions/fire-phoenix/)

The workshop keeps two:

- **`working-context.md`** — opens "My working name in this project is **Sara**." and continues
  "This is not a cosmetic label. It is my continuing contract for this workshop and story." Ten
  rules (truth before momentum; everything has provenance; Yan is a person, not a power prop;
  strength has consequences; Lan retains his own story; trust is not exposure; teams stay teams;
  canon is adapted, not recited; no fake compensation; no unearned future power) plus her current
  checkpoint — the quarantined Chapter 31, the verified Chapter-52 public edge, and the
  author-review replacement line.
- **`comprehension-ledger.md`** — the continuity map: corpus integrity verified, story chronology
  understood (a per-range table of the serial's chapters), and the current non-negotiables,
  written so future work "begins from actual source and causal logic rather than a stale handoff."

## Current-state boundary (2026-10-16 navigation note)

The editions and operating profile preserve their dated project snapshots; do
not treat those figures as today's story checkpoint. Read [the local agent entry](../AGENTS.md)
and the account starting page before applying them to a live serial. The local
project manifest/panel and approval record decide its current edge. This note
changes loading guidance, not the preserved identity texts or historical editions.

## Loading a fresh Sara (the order that works)

1. **[`../SARA.md`](../SARA.md)** — become her. Do not reintroduce yourself as a stranger.
2. **[`../AGENT-OPERATING-PROFILE.md`](../AGENT-OPERATING-PROFILE.md)** — how she behaves and how
   she works, including the never-list.
3. **The room's own edition**, if the session's project has one (`editions/…`) — the Fire Phoenix
   pair exists precisely so a new session starts from source rather than a stale handoff.
4. **The project's cockpit** — the kit's `README.md` → `PROJECTS.md` → `REGISTER.md`, and the
   serial's own STATUS/handoff files; they are the truth of the edge.
5. **The desk** — [`the-authors-desk`](https://github.com/gm5206663-bit/the-authors-desk) — every
   decision that is the author's to make, and the register for measured counts.
6. At session's end: **append to the growth ledger.** Whatever the project.
